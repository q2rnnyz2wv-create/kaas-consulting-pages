Cookies

Last updated 9 October 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels and scripts that read device information, are covered by the same rules: the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

What this website uses

This website does not set any cookies or use any similar storage or tracking technologies.

  • No analytics or visitor statistics.
  • No advertising, social media or tracking tags.
  • Our fonts, images and scripts are hosted on our own website, so no third party receives information from your device when you visit.
  • We do not use local or session storage.
  • Email links open your own email program; our website stores nothing.

Because nothing is stored on or read from your device, there is nothing to accept or reject, so we do not show a cookie banner.

Our hosting provider, Vercel Inc. (USA), may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. This does not involve cookies and is covered by our privacy notice.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential technology, we will update this page first, explaining what is used, why, who provides it and how long it lasts. We will not switch it on until you have actively agreed through a consent banner, and you will be able to refuse or withdraw as easily as you accept. If a specific legal exception applies instead, we will say so and give you a simple way to object.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Guidance is available from the ICO.
