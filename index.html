Our perspective

From the deal to the day it works.

We work on the two moments that most often go wrong: bringing companies together (or apart), and switching on major new technology. We plan it, pick the partners, then stay to make sure it works.

Programmes are led through operational staff or fully built transformation teams, from boardroom to frontline. Our engagements with large enterprises are about sustained delivery, not short-term projects.
