About the firm

Turning complex change into measurable results.

Kaas Consulting Limited is a transformation consultancy, leading strategy and delivery for global enterprises.

Who we are

Senior practitioners, close to delivery.

We bring global, cross-functional change experience across M&A integration, operating model design, SAP and AI/automation, in technology, media, FMCG, insurance, telecoms, data centres, manufacturing and oil & gas.

We set the strategy, benchmark the options and choose the partners, then stay close enough to the delivery to make sure it lands.

Our engagements run over multiple years with large enterprises. We lead through the client’s operational staff or fully built transformation teams, scaling to several hundred people, and we draw on a network of specialists in governance, change, data and technology where the work needs extra depth.
